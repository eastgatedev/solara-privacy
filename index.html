Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 25, 2026

Solara: Daily Ritual ("Solara", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Data stored locally on your device:

  • Language preference and app settings
  • Daily ritual records (emotion check-ins, gratitude entries)
  • Streak and usage statistics
  • Saved affirmations
  • Goal selections

In the current version, all data is stored locally on your device using encrypted local storage. We do not transmit this data to any server.

Data collected when you sign in (future update):

  • Email address (via Google Sign-in or Apple ID)
  • Display name
  • Your ritual data will be synced to secure cloud storage for backup purposes

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalize your daily ritual experience
  • To track your streak and display progress statistics
  • To generate AI-powered personalized affirmations (when using paid features)
  • To sync your data across devices (when signed in)

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. Your ritual entries, emotions, and gratitude journals are yours alone.

We may use anonymized, aggregated data to improve our app experience. This data cannot be used to identify you.

Third-Party Services

Solara may use the following third-party services in future updates:

  • Google Sign-in / Apple ID — for authentication only
  • Supabase — for secure cloud data storage and sync
  • Google Play Billing — for in-app credit purchases
  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — for generating personalized affirmations (no personal data is sent; only your selected goals and anonymized emotion data)

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. Local data is stored in encrypted storage on your device. Cloud data (in future updates) will be protected by row-level security, ensuring only you can access your own data.

Children's Privacy

Solara is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Deletion

You can delete all your local data by uninstalling the app. For cloud data (in future updates), you may request data deletion by contacting us at the email below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
